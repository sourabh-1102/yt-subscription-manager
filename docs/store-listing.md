## Summary (≤132 characters)
Organize, analyze and clean up your YouTube subscriptions. Free, private, local-first. No ads, no tracking servers.

## Category
Productivity → Tools

## Description

Take back control of hundreds of YouTube subscriptions. Everything runs privately in your browser.

📋 ALL YOUR SUBSCRIPTIONS IN ONE PLACE
• Search, sort and filter by category, watch activity and more
• Stays fast with 5,000+ channels

🗂️ CATEGORIES & FAVORITES
• Unlimited custom categories with emoji, and channels can be in several
• Drag channels onto categories, or move many at once
• Favorites, Review Later and private notes
• Organizing never changes your YouTube account

📊 WATCH ANALYTICS (optional, on-device)
• See which subscriptions you actually watch: most watched, last watched, weekly trends and category share
• Import your Google Takeout watch history to get past data on day one
• Turn it on or off any time, exclude channels, and choose how long history is kept

🧹 CLEANUP CENTER
• Find channels you never watch, channels you stopped watching, and channels that stopped uploading
• Review Assistant: you review every selected channel and confirm once, then it unsubscribes them for you
• Unsubscribed dashboard: re-subscribe in one click if you change your mind, and your categories come back too

🔔 BELL AUDIT
• Plan which channels should notify you (All / Personalized / None), then apply each one on YouTube with one click per channel and track your progress
• YouTube doesn't let apps change bells directly, so this is the honest way to do it

📦 YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
• Export or import a JSON backup at any time
• No account with us, no servers, no ads, no analytics SDKs, nothing sold
• Read-only Google access by default. Permission to manage subscriptions is requested only when you first confirm an unsubscribe.
